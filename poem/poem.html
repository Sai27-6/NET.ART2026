Dreams

By Langston Hughes

Hold fast to dreams
For if dreams die
Life is a broken-winged bird
That cannot fly.

Hold fast to dreams
For when dreams go
Life is a barren field
Frozen with snow.

Why I Like This Poem

  1. It reminds me to never stop dreaming. Also to keep trying my best in life.
  2. It is easy to interpret.
