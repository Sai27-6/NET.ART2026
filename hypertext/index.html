The Butterfly

The First Morning

A thin line of sunlight slipped through the cocoon. The butterfly pushed against its walls until the silk opened and the whole garden appeared at once.

The butterfly unfolded its new wings and began to explore the garden. It was full of beautiful flowers and buzzing insects which was so unfamiliar to the butterfly. It wondered if it truly belonged to the garden.

Three figures appeared nearby. Which one should the butterfly approach?

  1. Meet another butterfly resting on a sunflower.
  2. Meet a bee gathering pollen from a cluster of daisies.
  3. Meet a moth hiding beneath a broad leaf.
