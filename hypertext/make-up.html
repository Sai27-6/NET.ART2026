After the Argument

The butterfly stopped trying to win the argument and explained why the place choosen by the companion felt unsafe. Its companion then admitted that the dark hollow log felt frightening. Once they understood each other, they noticed a third choice: a wide leaf beside the log that was protected by the rosebush.

They waited beneath the leaf until the rain softened. The argument had not ended the friendship. Instead, it taught them that listening could reveal a path neither creature had seen alone.

When the clouds separated, voices rose from the wet grass. A group of unfamiliar bugs had come out to examine the shining garden.

  1. Go meet the other bugs together.
  2. Travel together toward the new garden beyond the trees.
