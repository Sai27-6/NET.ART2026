The New Garden

The butterfly passed through the trees and entered a field of unfamiliar flowers. Some grew close together. Others stood alone. A few leaned across the spaces between them, touching when the wind moved in the right direction.

The butterfly looked back at the path it had chosen. Friendship had brought welcome, disagreement, surprise, and change. Distance had sometimes protected it, but openness had also shown it possibilities it could not have discovered alone.

The butterfly wondered what would change if it had chosen a different path.
