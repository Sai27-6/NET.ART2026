Voices in the Grass

Near the edge of the garden, the butterfly found a ladybug, a cricket, and a firefly sharing stories beneath a fern. They did not all fly, eat the same food, or explore at the same time. Even so, they had made room for one another.

The butterfly hovered at the edge of the group wondering if it should say hello or move on. Neither choice guaranteed comfort, and neither choice had to be permanent.

  1. Say hello and join the conversation.
  2. Move on and search for a new garden.
