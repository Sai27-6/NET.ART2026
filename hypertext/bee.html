The Busy Bee

The bee moved quickly from flower to flower. At first, the butterfly worried that it was interrupting important work. Then the bee paused and pointed toward a patch of clover.

“The sweetest nectar is over there,” the bee said. “You can come with me, but I cannot stay in one place for long.”

The butterfly admired the bee’s confidence and knowledge. Still, it wondered whether two creatures with such different rhythms could enjoy the same journey.

What should the butterfly do?

  1. Follow the bee through the garden.
  2. Let the bee work and continue exploring.
