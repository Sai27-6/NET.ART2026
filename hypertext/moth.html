The Moth Beneath the Leaf

The moth remained in the shade while sunlight filled the rest of the garden. The butterfly asked why it was hiding.

“I am waiting for evening,” the moth replied. “The garden changes after sunset. Flowers close, stars appear, and quiet paths become easier to hear.”

The butterfly had never imagined the garden at night. The moth seemed kind, but waiting for darkness meant entering a world the butterfly did not yet understand.

What should the butterfly do?

  1. Stay with the moth and wait for evening.
  2. Say goodbye and continue exploring in daylight.
