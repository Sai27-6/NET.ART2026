The Sudden Storm

The two new friends stayed together until the bright sky turned gray. Wind bent the flowers, and rain began tapping against the leaves. They spotted shelter, but they disagreed about where to go.

The butterfly wanted to hide inside a hollow log. Its companion insisted going somewhere else. Both refused to compromise as the discussion turned into a fight and both flew off in different directions. The butterfly felt sad and lonely because it thought friendship was meant to be easy and without conflict.

The butterfly decided that it had to make a choice. It could either find their companion, or it could leave and find its own way forward.

  1. Listen, explain its own fear, and solve the disagreement together.
  2. Keep its distance and fly toward a new garden.
